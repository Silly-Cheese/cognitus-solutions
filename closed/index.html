A final word from Cognitus Solutions

If you followed an old link and ended up here, the page you were looking for is no longer accessible. All former Cognitus Solutions pages now redirect to this notice. All files hosted by Cognitus Solutions will be permanently deleted as part of the shutdown, and old download links will no longer provide access.

Before this becomes the only page anyone sees, I want to say a little about what used to be here.

Cognitus started with an idea for a Discord bot. People in Roblox communities could register, and others could look them up. The idea grew as I started thinking through what a lookup would actually need to tell you. How could an organization verify that a profile belonged to the right person? How could someone show where they had worked? What could they do if a record about them was wrong? Each of those questions gave me something else to work on. Eventually, the idea had grown into a place for profiles, organization verification, employment records, and appeals. Then came the staff portal, Cognitus Academy, Cognitus Pay, and Cognitus Careers. There was a point when I was working out how someone could ask a former employer for a reference. That’s a long way from the first idea of typing a name into Discord.

One of the smallest things I worked on is also one I’m especially glad I included. I wanted the Beta Tester badge to be a little test tube. There were badges for early supporters and bug hunters, too. I wanted a visible way to give people credit for the time they spent trying things out, pointing out what broke, and helping me see what I’d missed. A lot of that work happens quietly. It mattered to me that there was a place for it on someone’s profile.

There were parts of Cognitus I could have kept working on for a long time. I always had another idea for the site or something I wanted to go back and improve. That’s part of what makes it hard to sit down and write the last page. I’m going to miss having a new idea for Cognitus and getting to see how far I could take it. I’m proud that the bot went from an idea to actually running, and I’m proud of how much I learned between that first version and the last thing I worked on.

If you tested something, reported a bug, helped behind the scenes, or took an interest when Cognitus was still taking shape, thank you. And if you came back today because you remembered the name and wanted to see what became of it, I’m glad you got to read this. Cognitus Solutions is closed, and I’m grateful I got to build it and share it with you.

— Executive_Eagle (i_be_frank)
